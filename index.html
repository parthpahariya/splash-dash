A CAPTAIN’S CRASH COURSE

Own the open water.

Survive as long as you can. Your boat sails forward automatically through four lanes.

  • ← → or A D to change lanes.
  • Click or tap a pirate ship to aim and fire at it. Hold SPACE or the Fire button to shoot straight ahead. Two hits sink a pirate for 250 points.
  • Press ↑ to use a jump. You have two jumps per voyage; pickups do not replenish them.
  • Pirates aim every cannonball at your boat when they fire. Change lanes or press ↑ to jump over incoming shots. Rocks deal 25 damage, pirate shots 15, and ramming a pirate 30. A jump clears all three.
  • Pink hearts restore 10 health, up to 100.
  • Press P or ESC to pause. On touchscreens, use the buttons below the water.

The farther you sail, the faster the water gets. Keep an open lane in sight.
