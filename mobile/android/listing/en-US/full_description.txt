Welcome aboard The Last Sail!

Take the helm of a small boat on a sea full of rocks and pirates. Steer across four lanes, jump over rocks, fire your cannon at pirate ships and sail on to find your treasure.

SIMPLE TO PLAY
• Swipe left or right to change lanes.
• Swipe up to jump over rocks.
• Hold Fire to shoot ahead, or tap a pirate ship to aim at it.

PIRATES EVERYWHERE
Six kinds of pirate ships can show up at any time, each with its own way of attacking:
• Scouts fire quick single shots.
• Gunners fire steady cannon shots.
• Burst Raiders fire three shots in a row.
• Scattershots spray shots across the lanes.
• Sharpshooters mark their aim before they fire.
• Pirate Captains are tough and switch between bursts and spreads.
Read their attacks, find the gaps and sink them for points. Tougher ships are worth more.

KEEP YOUR BOAT AFLOAT
Rocks, cannonballs and ramming ships all damage your hull. Collect hearts to repair it. The water gets faster as you sail further, so stay sharp.

FIND YOUR TREASURE
Survive the voyage, beat what guards the treasure island and claim your treasure.

FEATURES
• Quick, one-handed arcade action
• Six pirate ship types with different attacks
• Your best score is saved
• Sound can be turned on or off
• A handbook that explains every rule
• Screen reader support for steering and jumping
