Privacy Policy

The Last Sail · Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short

The app collects nothing. Your best score and sound setting stay on your device. There are no ads, analytics or trackers, and no special permissions.

This policy explains how The Last Sail (“the app”), a pirate boat arcade game for Android and iOS, handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on a server or share any personal information. You don’t need an account to play, and the app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves two things on your own device: your best score and whether sound is on or off. This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions.

Third-party services

The app does not include analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, for example if advertising is added, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please email theboringmanapps@gmail.com.
