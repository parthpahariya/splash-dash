Simple to play

Rocks, cannonballs and ramming ships all damage your hull, and the water gets faster the further you sail.

Swipe to steer

Swipe left or right to change lanes and slip between rocks and cannon fire.

Swipe up to jump

Leap clean over rocks when there’s no gap to steer through.

Fire!

Hold Fire to shoot ahead, or tap a pirate ship to aim straight at it.

Stay afloat

Collect hearts to repair your hull and keep the voyage going.

Set sail

Read their attacks, find the gaps and sink them for points.

Pirates everywhere

Six kinds of pirate ship can show up at any time, each with its own way of attacking. Tougher ships are worth more.

  • Scout

    Fires quick single shots.

  • Gunner

    Fires steady cannon shots.

  • Burst Raider

    Fires three shots in a row.

  • Scattershot

    Sprays shots across the lanes.

  • Sharpshooter

    Marks its aim before it fires.

  • Pirate Captain Boss

    Tough, and switches between bursts and spreads.
